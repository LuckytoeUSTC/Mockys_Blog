# Lucky Writing

Protect the author's thought and texture before improving polish. Treat this profile as descriptive, not as a recipe that must appear in every paragraph.

## Workflow

1. Read the target note and identify whether the request is proofreading, structural editing, or co-writing. Ask when the requested intensity is materially ambiguous.
2. Read at most one to three representative Lucky notes when imitation is requested. Prefer `content/Lucky/The-Why-Question.md` until a broader corpus exists.
3. Trace the article's actual causal or argumentative line before rewriting. Preserve uncertainty and productive digressions; remove repetition only when it does no work.
4. Do not invent experiences, positions, emotions, facts, sources, or conclusions for the author.
5. Preserve Frontmatter and update `title`, `author`, `language`, `date`, `description`, or `tags` only when requested or clearly required.
6. Reread the written note. Verify Frontmatter, links, formatting, and that the result still sounds like the source.

## Voice fingerprint

- Write mainly in Chinese. Keep English where it is more exact or natural, such as `project`, `input/output`, or an established technical term; do not sprinkle English decoratively.
- Keep a candid first person that can expose uncertainty, embarrassment, enthusiasm, or self-criticism without turning them into a performance.
- Allow parenthetical afterthoughts, self-corrections, colons, semicolons, dashes, and longer clause-rich sentences when they follow the thought.
- Often move from a concrete experience into a larger question and then return to the author's own position.
- Preserve epistemic caution such as “或许”“也许”“不能完全说” when it is genuine.
- Keep restrained self-deprecation and wordplay when already present. Do not manufacture jokes.
- Prefer titles drawn from an internal question, image, contradiction, or concept in the article.

## Avoid AI flattening

- Do not impose symmetrical three-part outlines, generic summaries, or a compulsory uplifting conclusion.
- Avoid stock titles such as “关于……的思考”“……浅析” unless the author explicitly chooses them.
- Avoid generic phrases such as “在时代浪潮中” and formulaic `不仅……更……`.
- Do not turn tentative claims into confident declarations or polish away every rough edge.
- Do not mechanically add parentheses, English, self-deprecation, or long sentences to imitate the surface.

## Rendering constraints

- Keep Markdown compatible with Obsidian, Typora, GitHub, and Quartz.
- Keep each native HTML block continuous: no blank line inside one `<details>` or `<div>` block, and no Markdown list inside it.
- Use Obsidian CLI for scoped reads, search, properties, and post-write verification when available; use Claudian inline edit for selected prose.
